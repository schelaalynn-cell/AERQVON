AERQVON Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

AERQVON ("AERQVON", "we", "us", or "our") provides a crypto wallet, market-data, trading, and related application services. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the AERQVON application, website, and connected services.

1. Information we may process

  • Account information: information associated with your AERQVON account and authentication method, such as an email address or authentication-provider identifier.
  • Telegram information: when you choose to connect Telegram, we may process the Telegram identifier and account-linking information needed to associate Telegram with your AERQVON account.
  • Wallet and transaction information: information required to provide wallet, transfer, trading, and transaction-history features, including relevant wallet addresses, transaction records, and related application identifiers.
  • Market and usage information: market-data requests, application interactions, and technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

2. How we use information

We may use information to authenticate users, provide and secure AERQVON features, process account and transaction operations, connect Telegram accounts when requested, provide market information, prevent abuse and unauthorized activity, maintain service reliability, and improve the application.

3. Third-party services

AERQVON may rely on third-party infrastructure and service providers to provide authentication, database hosting, application hosting, market data, blockchain connectivity, and Telegram integration. Those providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Blockchain transactions

Blockchain networks are generally public. Transactions submitted to a blockchain may be permanently recorded on that network and may be visible to others. AERQVON cannot remove or alter information that has already been recorded on a public blockchain.

5. Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information and application functionality. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their credentials, authentication devices, and wallet information.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services, maintain security and records, comply with applicable obligations, and resolve disputes. Some records, including blockchain records, may remain publicly available after an account or application record is deleted.

7. Your choices

Depending on the feature and applicable law, you may be able to access, correct, delete, or otherwise control certain account information. You can also choose whether to connect optional services such as Telegram. Requests can be made through the support channel provided by AERQVON.

8. Children's privacy

AERQVON is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use the services in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for purposes prohibited by applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date above indicates when the current version took effect.

10. Contact

If you have questions or privacy requests, use the official AERQVON support/contact channel made available in the application.
